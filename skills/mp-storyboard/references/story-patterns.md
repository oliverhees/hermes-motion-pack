# Story patterns with timings

## A. Problem → Turn → Payoff → Proof → CTA (product spot, 30 s)
0–3 hook (pain in ≤ 5 words) · 3–8 tension (the old way, cluttered, slow) · 8–11 turn (product enters, one gesture) · 11–22 payoff (3 features as story moments, each ≤ 4 s) · 22–27 proof (number, logo wall, quote) · 27–30 CTA + logo

## B. Hook → 3 beats → Punch (reel, 15 s)
0–1.5 hook (claim/question) · 1.5–11 three beats of ~3 s (1. ... 2. ... 3. ...) each with one big word + one small line · 11–13 punch (the twist/result) · 13–15 CTA / loop back to hook

## C. Reveal (teaser, 8 s)
0–2 darkness with motion (particles, light) · 2–5 fragments assemble · 5–7 wordmark/date lands with hit · 7–8 hold + url

## D. Before/After (reel, 12 s)
0–1 "before" label · 1–5 messy state with annoying motion · 5–6 flash/wipe · 6–10 clean state, calm motion · 10–12 one line + CTA

## E. Number story (data, 20 s)
0–2 the question · 2–6 counter climbs to the number (hero) · 6–12 the comparison (bars/lines) · 12–17 what it means (one sentence) · 17–20 source + CTA

## F. Logo sting (4 s, loopable)
0–0.3 anticipation (tiny scale-down) · 0.3–1.2 parts fly in with springs · 1.2–2.0 settle + light sweep · 2.0–3.5 hold with idle · 3.5–4.0 return to start state (loop) or exit
