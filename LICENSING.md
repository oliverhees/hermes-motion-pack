scripts, not your output.

## FAQ

**May I use the pack for client videos?**
Yes. Making videos for clients is use, not distribution of the pack.

**May I call a fork "hermes-motion-pack" or use "AIIANER"?**
No. Trademarks (see README, section "Trademarks") are not part of the code
license. Forks use their own name.

**HyperFrames, GSAP, ffmpeg?**
Separate projects with their own licences (see NOTICE). The pack only calls them.
